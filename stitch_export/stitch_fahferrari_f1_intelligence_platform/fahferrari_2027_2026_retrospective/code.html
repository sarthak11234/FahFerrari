SECTION 03 // ARCHITECTURAL POST-MORTEM

5 Critical Lessons Learned & Bayesian Remediations

SYSTEMIC DEFECTS RESOLVED IN PRODUCTION ENGINE v2027
VULN #01FALSE PRECISION

The Pseudo-Confidence Interval Fallacy

Impact Severity
CRITICAL (HIGH RISK)
Legacy Defect in v2026.1cancel

Treated the disagreement spread among 4 tree models (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, RF) as a statistical confidence interval.

Root Pathology: When all 4 trees share the same training feature bias, ensemble variance collapses toward zero, producing artificial certainty.
Bayesian Resolution in v2027check_circle

Replaced with true Bayesian hierarchical MCMC posterior sampling (10,000 paired draws, Gelman-Rubin R-hat < 1.008).

Mathematical Gain: Generates mathematically rigorous Highest Density Intervals (HDI) reflecting real epistemological uncertainty.
VULN #02COVARIANCE BIAS

Rigid Driver Points Multipliers & Independent Marginals

Impact Severity
HIGH SEVERITY
Legacy Defect in v2026.1cancel

Derived individual driver points via static percentage split heuristics, treating teammates as statistically independent draws.

Root Pathology: Zero-sum track physics violated; simulated seasons allowed Ferrari to score P1 and P2 more often than physically admissible.
Bayesian Resolution in v2027check_circle

Joint bivariate normal posterior sampling with empirical paired intra-team covariance (ρ = +0.62).

Mathematical Gain: Strict zero-sum finishing constraints preserved across all 10,000 Monte Carlo stochastic season iterations.
VULN #03CIRCUIT BLINDNESS

Coarse Season Aggregations & Loss of Circuit Granularity

Impact Severity
HIGH SEVERITY
Legacy Defect in v2026.1cancel

Compressed annual team performance into static seasonal averages, wiping out micro-sector and circuit-specific track traits.

Root Pathology: Missed Ferrari's mechanical low-speed grip edge at Monaco and Monza while overestimating them on high-speed sweepers like Silverstone.
Bayesian Resolution in v2027check_circle

Micro-sector simulation across 5 distinct aerodynamic circuit archetypes ingested directly from FastF1 10Hz cornering telemetry.

Mathematical Gain: Accurately weights corner speed deltas per aerodynamic downforce envelope.
VULN #04DATA LEAKAGE

Look-Ahead Bias & Backward-Filling in Data Pipelines

Impact Severity
SYSTEMIC LEAK
Legacy Defect in v2026.1cancel

Unintentionally leaked late-season aerodynamic upgrade metrics and year-end points pace backward into preseason training features.

Root Pathology: Inflated backtesting accuracy in offline training while creating severe generalization error in real out-of-sample preseason forecasts.
Bayesian Resolution in v2027check_circle

Point-in-time cryptographic manifests, rolling-origin cross-validation, and an immutable pre-testing freeze anchor (T0 = Feb 18, 2027).

Mathematical Gain: Strict zero-leakage training guarantee enforced by SHA-256 dataset hashing.
VULN #05STOCHASTIC MISS

Uncalibrated Random Race Order Distributions

Impact Severity
MODERATE DRIFT
Legacy Defect in v2026.1cancel

Applied heuristic uniform random noise to generate finishing orders rather than probabilistic survival hazards and track position pace curves.

Root Pathology: Severely miscalculated safety car impact windows, wet-weather lap variance, and empirical DNF clustering.
Bayesian Resolution in v2027check_circle

Weibull parametric hazard survival curves (k = 1.24, λ = 0.041) for power unit degradation and empirical stochastic safety-car transition matrices.
